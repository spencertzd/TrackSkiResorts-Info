# FreshTrax Support

Welcome to the official support page for **FreshTrax**, an app that helps you log the ski resorts you’ve visited, rate your experiences, and explore community resort lists and ratings.

## Need Help?
If you run into any issues with the app or have questions:

- 📧 Contact: [freshtraxsupport@gmail.com](mailto:freshtraxsupport@gmail.com)
- 🧾 Privacy Policy: [FreshTrax Privacy Policy](https://spencertzd.github.io/TrackSkiResorts-Info/)

## Features
- Track ski resorts you’ve visited
- Rate your experience (1–3 scale)
- Save personal notes and favorite runs
- View community resort lists and ratings
- With optional Premium: record ski days, explore maps and statistics, manage season passes, and import or export GPX files

Written resort notes and favorite runs are not shared on the public leaderboard.

## Feedback
Have a suggestion for FreshTrax? Email [freshtraxsupport@gmail.com](mailto:freshtraxsupport@gmail.com) with your ideas or feedback.
